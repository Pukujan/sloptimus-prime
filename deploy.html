Need detail instead? See the tech specs.

How long is the trial, really?

14 days, full reasoning core, no card required. Extend once by asking your solutions engineer.

Can it touch production data?

Not during trial. Write access unlocks after shadow mode passes your review, day 17 at the earliest.

What if it gets something wrong?

Every write action carries a diff, an approver and a one-click rollback. Guardrails are yours to set.

Do we need ML engineers?

No. A solutions engineer handles tuning; your team reviews outputs and approves the runbook.

Can we run it in our own VPC?

Yes, on Fleet tier — self-hosted inference with the same control plane.

What does it integrate with?

REST, gRPC, webhooks, S3, Postgres, Salesforce, Jira, Slack and your internal APIs via the transform SDK.
