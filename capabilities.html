Workflow

How he transforms.

  1. 1

    Scan

    reads every input, maps structure, flags the damage.

  2. 2

    Plan

    selects a loadout of tools and models matched to the job, not the prompt.

  3. 3

    Transform

    executes the work in parallel passes, checking each against the plan.

  4. 4

    Verify

    runs your rules as a gate; anything short of spec goes back through.

  5. 5

    Deliver

    ships finished, deployable output with a full provenance record.

Scan, plan, transform, verify, deliver — the same five beats on every job, from a single CSV to a full platform migration.
